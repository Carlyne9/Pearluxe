### Polène: https://eng.polene-paris.com/collections/handbags
Closest match to Pearluxe's look (minimal, premium, leather in muted tones).
- **What we take:**
  - Strip of collection thumbnails above the grid (scrollable on mobile)
  - Product card: name as a heading, plus a quiet "edition" line (e.g. "Textured Camel"); price shown plainly
  - Two images per product: front, then side/turned view (shown on hover on desktop, swipe on touch)
  - Filter panel: sort, in-stock toggle, material, color, size, live "Show products • 308" count
  - Restrained UI copy, lots of whitespace
- **What we skip:** country/currency selector, store locator

### Winnie & Co: https://winnieandco.com
- **What we take:**
  - Announcement bar (e.g. delivery or free-shipping message)
  - Large portrait category tiles with uppercase labels (Shoulder, Tote, Mini…)
  - Product card: portrait image (~5:6), lifestyle photo shown on hover, "New" badge
  - Strip of 4 value points ("Premium leather", "Fast delivery"…)
  - Reviews section tied to specific products
  - Cart drawer with a free-delivery progress message
  - Newsletter sign-up with a first-order discount
- **What we skip:** the image-only hero. Pearluxe uses video there.

### Mytheresa: https://www.mytheresa.com/int/en/women/bags
Page renders with JavaScript, so this entry is from my general knowledge of the site, not this fetch.
- **What we take:** strict black-on-white editorial grid, bold uppercase brand/label line on cards, filters sitting next to the grid on desktop, mega menu
- **What we skip:** designer-name line (single brand), data-dense filter list

### The Bag Shop GH: https://www.thebagshopgh.com/shop
- **What we take:**
  - **Color:** plum/magenta brand color (`#9E1E65`, from their logo "O" and silhouette) → basis for our purple range
  - Header: logo centered, nav split left/right in wide-tracked caps
  - Product titles in a high-contrast serif on the product page
  - Color swatches on cards and product page; selected swatch gets a ring
  - Sale price in brand color + struck-through old price (`GH₵ 290.00 ~~GH₵ 300.00~~`)
  - Product page: gallery with arrows + thumbnails, "Select color", quantity stepper, Add to cart + wishlist heart, description, customer reviews
- **What we skip:** product codes as names (`PEDRA 63028#`), text burned into product photos, full-width Add to cart under every grid card
- **Screenshots:** `assets/references/the-bag-shop-gh/`

### Just Handbags GH: https://justhandbagsgh.com/products
- **What we take (local-market patterns, not visual style):**
  - Prices as `GHS 140` (no decimals)
  - Category tiles: Backpacks, Clutches, Handbags, Purses, Totes, Travel
  - WhatsApp contact link, Instagram + TikTok icons, delivery and returns policy pages
  - Physical location and opening hours in the footer
- **What we skip:** product codes as titles, square 1:1 images, full-width "ADD TO CART" on every card (looks wholesale)
